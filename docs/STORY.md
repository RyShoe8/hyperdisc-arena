# HyperDisc Arena: Story Mode

## The pitch

A Top Gun pilot's dog takes a HyperDisc for him on a Southern California beach. To get a shot at the man who threw it, Mick Magnum has to climb the HyperDisc gang's ladder one member at a time, in their own hangouts. He beats the champion and wins his revenge. Then he gets something he didn't expect: an invitation to the HyperDisc Arena League.

Story mode is the game's learning curve. Each opponent teaches one part of the game. Beating Keno "earns" you the big show, which is multiplayer. Online play is open from the start, but the story is the road there.

**Tone:** over-the-top seriousness played completely straight. Dry comedy, 80s action-movie energy and a lot of pop culture. Mick treats a frisbee ladder like a classified mission. Everyone else treats him like he's exhausting. Tango's injury is the one thing nobody jokes about.

## The ladder

Keno only plays challengers with a full **challenger card**: one stamp from each ranked member below him, collected in order from sixth up to second. The gang invented the card to keep people away from Keno. It ends up being Mick's map.

## Chapter 1: A Solo Tango

*Cutscene: the beach.*

Mick is a Top Gun fighter pilot by trade, but his heart belongs to the ocean. When he isn't taking down Russkies in his F-16, he spends his time at the beach in Southern California with his best friend Pete Pelican and his dog Tango (his real best friend). He has a crush on a lifeguard named Tiffany Seasons. Being a Top Gun pilot, he always misses his chance to ask her out, and Pete never lets him forget it.

One day Mick, Pete and Tango are at the beach, enjoying the view, surfing and reminiscing about the time they went inverted over a Russian MiG and flipped him the bird. They keep throwing Tango his ball, and he keeps fetching it back, tail wagging as he waits for the next throw.

Mick keeps glancing at Tiffany all day. She's up on her lifeguard tower, watching the ocean and glistening in the sun. *(Take-my-breath-away music on our first long look at her. Even Tango gets excited.)*

**Pete:** "Go talk to her, man! You miss 100% of the shots you don't take. You're a hero, Mick. Remember that time over Korea when I was out of fuel, and you tilted your plane into mine so just enough fuel got into my tank to make it home? You saved my life that day, and I will never forget it. Take Tango with you, bro. You know ladies can't resist this cute little troublemaker."

Mick starts toward Tiffany's tower. Before he gets there, a group of six walks up and starts talking to her. As he gets closer, he can see they're teasing her. One of them has taken her whistle and she's trying to get it back. Mick's pilot instincts kick in. He senses trouble brewing. Tango keeps tight to his side.

**Mick:** "Do you all have a problem? Because it looks like you're picking on the lifeguard."

**Keno:** "What's it to you, pretty boy? You trying to get into her pants? Better wait your turn, because I'm getting there first."

**Mick** *(blushing)***:** "Don't talk about her that way! Show her some respect and give her back her whistle, you losers. Or there's gonna be high-altitude trouble." *(Tango growls.)*

**Steve:** "Hey, Keno! This guy thinks he's some kind of hero. Look at him, with that haircut and those shades. What are you, some type of movie character?"

**Mick:** "I'm a pilot, and I've served my entire life. As a baby, on the national tricycle Olympic team. In elementary school, as leader of the national hall monitor association. In high school, as a state champion soccer player. In college, as leader of the Air Force Reserves. And now, in Korea, in Russia, in Poland, in Brazil... as the Captain of Top Gun. The most elite Air Force unit in existence, protecting the weak and promoting freedom all over the world!"

*Cut to all six gang members looking bored and half asleep. Then they all burst out laughing.*

**Gang:** "Haha, what a chump!" / "This guy's submissive to authority." / "Is your mom your co-pilot too?" / "What a sucker." / "I zoned out, but this guy is hot." / "I'd never want to have a beer with this guy." / "I'm not crying, you're crying."

**Keno:** "Look, flyboy, the lady isn't interested. She's got bigger goals in mind. This airspace is too much of a danger zone for you. We wouldn't want to see you... crash and burn."

**Mick:** "You think you can take me, tough guy? You probably can't even spell your own name. Walk away, all of you, or I'm going six-G target lock."

**Jason:** "Stop toying with him, Keno, and let's get out of here. We've got a reservation with a bar stool, and Alex is buying."

**Alex:** "I'm not buying. You haven't paid your last ten bills!"

**Jimmy:** "Well, it's hard to pay bills when you show up late for your roofing job every day, because your primary job of partying keeps you out all night!"

**Pete:** "That explains the roof at your bar."

**Alex:** "It explains the buckets at my bar."

*While Steve is distracted, Tiffany snatches her whistle back.*

**Tiffany:** "There. Everybody's had their fun. Go bother a parking meter."

*Mick steps between her and Keno.*

**Mick:** "You heard the lady."

**Tiffany:** "I was doing pretty well before the speech."

**Mick:** "Absolutely. I was providing air support."

*Keno looks down at Tango, who has dropped his tennis ball at Mick's feet.*

**Keno:** "Cute. You two practice fetching together?"

*Jimmy laughs before catching himself. Keno notices.*

*Keno takes the HyperDisc from under his arm. Its rim gives off a rising electric hum.*

**Alex:** "Put that away, Keno."

**Keno:** "Relax. The captain's got great reflexes."

*He whips the disc straight at Mick. Mick has barely begun to turn when Tango leaps.*

*A flash. A sharp metallic crack. The disc ricochets into the lifeguard sign and splits it in half. Tango falls into the sand behind the tower.*

*The sound drops away.*

**Mick:** "Tango?"

*Mick kneels beside him. Tango doesn't move.*

**Mick:** "Come on, buddy."

*His tennis ball rolls down the churned-up sand and stops against Mick's hand. Tiffany drops down beside them and checks Tango, while Pete grabs a towel from the tower.*

**Mick:** "Tiff...?"

**Tiffany:** "Pete, bring my truck around."

**Mick:** "He's not moving."

**Tiffany:** "We need to go. Right now."

*Pete and Tiffany carefully lift Tango onto the towel. His collar slips loose. Mick catches it. As they hurry to the truck, Mick stays kneeling in the sand, holding the collar. He believes he has just lost his best friend.*

*Behind him, Steve breaks the silence.*

**Steve:** "Technically, the dog entered the flight path."

**Vanessa** *(turning on him)***:** "Shut up."

*Even Jason looks shaken. Keno forces a shrug.*

**Keno:** "Wasn't aiming at the dog."

*Mick stands.*

**Mick:** "You should start running."

*Vanessa steps between them, facing Keno.*

**Vanessa:** "Leave. Now. You smelly losers."

*The gang retreats along the boardwalk.*

## Chapter 2: Happy Hour of Reckoning

**Opponent:** Alex Aloha (6th) · **Court:** The Leaky Tiki, the backyard court · **Lesson:** the basics

*The Leaky Tiki. A dingy beach bar with a palm-frond ceiling and three patrons, one of them a cat. Buckets are spaced across the floor at strategic intervals, each catching a slow, steady drip.*

*The door opens. Mick stands silhouetted in the doorway, Tango's collar wrapped around his wrist. Pete is behind him, still holding the car keys. They've come straight from the vet's waiting room.*

**Alex** *(not looking up)***:** "We're closed."

**Pete:** "The sign says open."

**Alex:** "The sign also says 'Waterproof'."

*A drip lands in a bucket. Mick sits at the bar.*

**Mick:** "Where's Keno?"

**Alex:** "Mick, right? I'm sorry about your dog. Honestly. That's not... We're bad, but we're not *that* kind of bad. Usually we're 'skip the tab' bad."

**Mick:** "Where. Is. Keno."

*Alex sighs, sets down the glass, and pulls a laminated card from under the register.*

**Alex:** "You can't just walk up to Keno. Nobody can. He only plays challengers with a full card. One win against each of us, bottom of the ladder to the top. Six, five, four, three, two. Then him."

**Pete:** "Who came up with that?"

**Alex:** "Steve. To keep people away from Keno."

**Pete:** "Does it work?"

**Alex:** "Nobody's ever finished it."

**Mick:** "Who's sixth?"

*Alex looks at the cat. The cat looks at Alex.*

**Alex:** "...I'm sixth."

**Mick:** "Then we start here."

**Alex:** "Out back. On the court. No cheap shots, and nobody else gets hurt."

**Mick:** "Agreed. And Alex? Pour me a milk. I'll drink it when we're done."

**Pete:** "He's very serious about milk."

### After the match

*Alex stamps the card with a tiki-shaped stamp.*

**Alex:** "Jimmy's next. He's at the rooftop party above the boardwalk. There's always a party on that roof, mostly because nobody can get the door to lock."

*Mick turns to leave.*

**Alex:** "For what it's worth... I hope your dog makes it."

*Mick stops, but doesn't turn around.*

**Mick:** "So do I."

*He drinks the milk in one go and leaves. Alex watches the door close, then looks at the cat.*

**Alex:** "I'm gonna go with him, aren't I."

**UNLOCKED: Alex Aloha**

## Chapter 3: Raise the Roof

**Opponent:** Jimmy Navio (5th) · **Court:** Boardwalk rooftop party · **Lesson:** curves and finesse

*Pete catches up with Mick on the hotel's back stairs.*

**Mick:** "Any news on Tango?"

**Pete:** "Tiffany's with him. They're working on him. She'll call the second she knows anything."

*Mick looks down at the collar on his wrist.*

**Pete:** "Until then you're stuck with your second-best friend."

**Mick:** "Third. He likes the mailman."

*The rooftop party. Neon, a DJ and a hot tub. A crooked canopy sags over the dance floor, and a bucket catches the water dripping through it. Jimmy is holding court beneath a flickering sign.*

**Pete** *(looking at the canopy)***:** "Beautiful work."

**Jimmy:** "Temporary installation."

**Pete:** "The bucket?"

**Jimmy:** "The roof."

*Jimmy spots the stamped challenger card and his smile wobbles.*

**Jimmy:** "Alex actually *played* you?"

**Mick:** "You're next."

**Jimmy:** "Look, man... what Keno did was way out of line."

**Mick:** "You walked away with him."

*Jimmy looks at the floor. Then the party notices the challenge, a crowd gathers, and his confidence comes back like a DJ drop.*

**Jimmy:** "Alright, Captain. Let's see if those hands can do anything besides salute."

### After the match

**Jimmy** *(stamping the card)***:** "You know, you could've just asked me where Jason was."

**Mick:** "I need the stamp."

**Jimmy:** "Right. The bureaucracy."

**Pete:** "You guys invented it."

*Jimmy grabs his jacket.*

**Jimmy:** "Jason's at the Last Stop. I'm coming with you."

**Mick:** "Why?"

**Jimmy:** "Because walking away once was enough. Also, I'm not here when this roof goes."

*Behind them, the canopy gives way. Somebody's hot tub gets a lot fuller.*

**UNLOCKED: Jimmy Navio**

## Chapter 4: NightTrain Has No Brakes

**Opponent:** Jason NightTrain (4th) · **Court:** The Last Stop, loading yard · **Lesson:** power, knockback and quick returns

*Behind the Last Stop, the worst dive bar on the coast. Jason is in the loading yard, curling a keg while staring at his reflection in a dark window.*

**Jimmy:** "You know that one's empty."

**Jason:** "I'm warming up."

*He sets it down much too carefully. Then he sees Mick.*

**Jason:** "Keno told me you'd come."

**Mick:** "Did he tell you why?"

**Jason:** "He said you can't take a joke."

*Nobody laughs.*

**Jimmy:** "He hit the dog, Jason."

**Jason:** "I was there."

**Jimmy:** "Then stop letting him tell you what happened."

*Jason looks at the card. Three stamps.*

**Jason:** "You want my spot, you earn it."

**Mick:** "I don't want your spot. I want Keno."

**Jason:** "Everybody wants Keno. That's why he's number one."

**Pete:** "I don't think you're hearing yourself."

**Jason:** "I hear myself fine. I do all of Keno's voices."

### After the match

*Jason sits on the empty keg, breathing hard. His phone rings. Keno's voice is loud enough for the whole yard to hear.*

**Keno:** "Tell me you stopped him."

**Jason:** "It was a really close—"

**Keno:** "You had one job."

**Jason:** "Actually, I've been covering three of your—"

**Keno:** "Put Jimmy on. Let me talk to someone useful."

*Jason looks at Jimmy. Then he hangs up and stamps the card.*

**Jason:** "Vanessa's training at Muscle Pier."

**Jimmy:** "Come with us."

**Jason:** "I'm not joining some friendship parade."

*He picks up his jacket and follows them anyway.*

**Pete:** "Of course not."

**Jason:** "We just happen to be walking in the same direction."

**UNLOCKED: Jason NightTrain**

## Chapter 5: Heavy Lifting

**Opponent:** Vanessa Trunks (3rd) · **Court:** Muscle Pier, at sunset · **Lesson:** lobs, specials and the power meter

*Muscle Pier at sunset. Vanessa is finishing a set of squats that would register on a seismograph. She sees Mick arrive with Jimmy, Jason and Alex trailing behind him like a very sheepish boy band.*

**Vanessa:** "That was fast."

**Jimmy:** "We're exploring other leadership opportunities."

*Mick holds out the card.*

**Mick:** "I need your match."

**Vanessa:** "And then Steve. And then Keno."

**Mick:** "That's the mission."

**Vanessa:** "You have a plan for after?"

*Mick doesn't answer. She notices Tango's collar on his wrist.*

**Vanessa:** "I should've stopped him."

**Mick:** "Yeah."

*She accepts that.*

**Vanessa:** "You'll get your match. But we keep it on the court."

*Pete studies a framed sponsorship photo by the entrance: Vanessa and Keno, both flexing, both oiled.*

**Pete:** "So are you two...?"

**Vanessa:** "That photo came with the sponsorship."

**Jimmy:** "Keno keeps a copy in his wallet."

**Vanessa:** "Keno keeps a photo of *himself* in his wallet."

### After the match

*Vanessa stamps the card.*

**Vanessa:** "When power stops working, he gets angry. When he gets angry, he gets careless."

**Mick:** "Sounds familiar."

**Vanessa:** "Good. You're listening."

*Pete's phone rings. Tiffany. He steps aside, listens, and closes his eyes.*

**Pete:** "Okay. Okay. Thank you."

*Mick is already beside him.*

**Mick:** "What?"

**Pete:** "He's alive, Mick. He's stable."

*Mick sits down on the nearest bench, because his legs have stopped taking orders. Pete hands him the phone.*

**Tiffany** *(on the phone)***:** "He's not out of the woods yet, but they think he's going to be okay."

**Mick:** "Tell him... tell him I've got his ball."

**Tiffany:** "I will."

*He hangs up. Pete sits down next to him.*

**Pete:** "We can stop, you know."

*Mick turns the collar over in his hands.*

**Mick:** "He threw that thing at me with a whole beach full of people behind me. Next time, Tango won't be there to stop it."

*Vanessa shoulders her bag.*

**Vanessa:** "Then let's make sure there isn't a next time."

## Chapter 6: Subject to Approval

**Opponent:** Steve Keeves (2nd) · **Court:** The HyperDome arcade lounge · **Lesson:** defence: slaps, pop ups and drop shots

*Inside the HyperDome, behind the arcade cabinets, is an office. A sign over the desk reads CHALLENGER RELATIONS. Steve sits beneath it, stamping things that don't need stamping.*

*He looks up at Mick and the increasingly large group behind him.*

**Steve:** "Do you have an appointment?"

*Mick puts the card on the desk.*

**Mick:** "Four stamps. You're number two."

*Steve inspects the card with a jeweler's loupe.*

**Steve:** "These are in blue ink."

**Pete:** "So?"

**Steve:** "Challenger cards require black."

**Alex:** "You gave me that pen."

**Steve** *(smiling without warmth)***:** "Alex. Lovely. We're all here."

*He slides a document across the desk.*

**Steve:** "Sign this and we can avoid a great deal of unpleasantness."

**Pete** *(reading over Mick's shoulder)***:** "This says Mick accepts full responsibility for the incident."

**Steve:** "Standard release."

**Pete:** "It says the dog was acting on his instructions."

**Steve:** "Page four."

**Mick:** "You get one chance to move that paper."

*Steve pulls it back.*

**Steve:** "Competitive people. Always so emotional."

**Vanessa** *(pointing at the court)***:** "Play him."

### After the match

*Steve loses, and immediately edges toward the scoring console.*

**Steve:** "Small technical correction."

**Pete** *(blocking him)***:** "Score looks right to me."

**Steve:** "You're not authorized to—"

*Alex turns the monitor around. Steve has already opened a form titled VOID CHALLENGE (FORM 27-B).*

*Silence.*

**Jimmy:** "How many times have you done that?"

*Steve looks around for an ally. Jason folds his arms. Vanessa holds out the challenger card. Steve stamps it.*

*Pete submits the result. The announcer booms through the building.*

**Announcer:** "A NEW CHALLENGER HAS QUALIFIED FOR TONIGHT'S CHAMPIONSHIP!"

*Outside, the crowd erupts.*

**Steve:** "I was going to announce that."

**Pete:** "We could tell."

## Chapter 7: Final Approach

**Opponent:** Keno Dice (champion) · **Court:** The HyperDome · **Lesson:** everything, under pressure

*Floodlights sweep the arena. Keno enters through smoke and fireworks, to a rock anthem that is mostly guitar solo.*

**Announcer:** "THE REIGNING CHAMPION! UNBEATEN POWERHOUSE! ENTREPRENEUR! CERTIFIED FITNESS PROFESSIONAL! KENO... DICE!"

**Pete:** "They left out 'dog enthusiast'."

**Mick:** "Pete."

**Pete:** "Yeah. Sorry."

*Keno sees his gang standing in Mick's corner.*

**Keno:** "What are you doing over there?"

**Jason:** "Walking in the same direction."

**Vanessa:** "He earned the match."

**Keno:** "Then he can lose it."

*Mick walks toward the court and stops at the tunnel entrance. He looks at the collar on his wrist. The crowd noise fades.*

**Mick:** "This one's for you, buddy."

### After the final match

*The final point lands. A moment of silence. Then the arena explodes.*

*Keno stares at the scoreboard. He picks up the disc and grips it hard. The rim starts to hum.*

**Mick:** "You've still got a choice."

*Vanessa steps to the edge of the court.*

**Vanessa:** "Put it down."

*The crowd has gone quiet. Nobody is cheering for him now. Keno lets the disc drop.*

**Steve** *(sidling up to Mick)***:** "As your new management representative—"

**Alex:** "No."

*Pete lifts Mick's arm. Jason lifts the other one, a little too enthusiastically.*

**Mick:** "Shoulder. Shoulder!"

*They put him down. Then a bark echoes through the arena.*

*At the tunnel entrance stands Tiffany. Beside her on a short lead, bandaged and moving carefully, but very much alive, is Tango.*

*Mick drops to his knees. Tango limps across the court, the crowd parting for him, and presses his head into Mick's chest.*

**Mick** *(barely holding it together)***:** "Hey, wingman."

*Tango licks his chin. For a moment, the arena disappears. Tiffany kneels beside them.*

**Tiffany:** "He's supposed to be resting. He heard the crowd on the TV in the waiting room and wouldn't stop barking until we came."

**Mick:** "Thank you."

**Tiffany:** "Thank me by getting through one sporting event without anybody needing medical attention."

*Mick takes the tennis ball out of his pocket and puts it down in front of Tango. Tango picks it up. Tail going.*

*Keno, who has been standing very still, walks over. Everyone tenses. He crouches down, a respectful distance from the dog.*

**Keno:** "...I'm sorry, little guy."

*Tango considers this, and keeps his ball.*

**Tiffany:** "So. Were you going to talk to me this morning?"

**Mick:** "That was the original mission."

**Tiffany:** "How was it supposed to go?"

*Mick takes a breath.*

**Mick:** "Would you like to have dinner with me?"

**Tiffany:** "Yes."

*Mick looks briefly stunned.*

**Pete:** "Unbelievable. The direct approach."

## Finale: The Big Show

*The arena is emptying. Confetti is still falling. A figure in a crisp white suit and mirrored sunglasses walks onto the court, flanked by two men carrying briefcases that are much too large.*

**The Commissioner:** "Mick Magnum. Quite a run."

**Mick:** "Who's asking?"

**The Commissioner:** "The HyperDisc Arena League. The real thing. Pro courts, sold-out domes, players from every coast, every country, every time zone."

*One of the briefcases opens. Inside, on velvet, is a single gold challenger card.*

**The Commissioner:** "Keno's been our regional champion for three years. We kept waiting for someone to beat him. We'd like to invite you to the League."

**Steve:** "I can negotiate the—"

**Everyone:** "*No.*"

*Mick takes the card. He looks at Pete, at Tiffany, at the gang, and at Tango, who is chewing the corner of the Commissioner's briefcase.*

**Mick:** "When do I start?"

**The Commissioner:** "Whenever you're ready. The League never closes."

*He walks off into the floodlights. The briefcase men follow. One of them is missing a corner.*

**Pete:** "So, a whole League full of people who throw these things at your head."

**Mick:** "Pete, I've been training my entire life for this. As a baby, on the national tricycle Olympic team—"

**Pete:** "Nope."

**THE HYPERDISC ARENA LEAGUE AWAITS**

## Post-credits: A New Season

*Several weeks later. The beach court has reopened for a community tournament.*

*Alex runs the refreshments stand. A cat sleeps on his cash register. Jimmy and Jason are fixing the canopy.*

**Jason:** "Is this level?"

**Jimmy** *(tilting his head)***:** "From here."

*Vanessa takes the hammer away from both of them.*

*Steve sits at a folding registration table beneath a handwritten sign: NO ADMINISTRATION FEES. Keno arrives carrying equipment. Vanessa points at a stack of barriers. He considers arguing, then starts unloading.*

*At the lifeguard tower, Tiffany checks her watch.*

**Tiffany:** "Seven o'clock, Mick. No uniform."

**Mick:** "Casual, or tactical casual?"

**Tiffany:** "A shirt."

*Pete throws Tango his tennis ball. Fully recovered, Tango watches it bounce away. Instead, he picks up a training HyperDisc, trots onto the court, drops it at the serving line, and barks.*

*Mick looks at Pete. Pete looks at Tiffany.*

**Pete:** "Your best friend wants a promotion."

*Freeze frame on Tango, tail wagging.*

**TANGO UNLOCKED**
*Good boy. Bad matchup.*

Mick, Pete, Tiffany, Vanessa, Steve and Keno are in the roster from the start. Multiplayer stays open the whole time; the story is the guided road to it, not a gate.

## Production notes

- **New courts:**
  - The Leaky Tiki backyard
  - Boardwalk rooftop
  - The Last Stop loading yard
  - Muscle Pier at sunset
  - HyperDome arcade lounge
  - The HyperDome (could reuse Stadium with HyperDome branding)
- **New characters:** Alex, Jimmy, Jason, Tango. The Commissioner appears in the finale only, so he can be a portrait with no playable sprite.
- **Lessons:** each chapter's lesson can show as a one-line tip before the match, for example "Alex plays it safe: catch, aim and use the walls". The CPU for each opponent should lean on that chapter's skill, so the lesson is something you play, not something you read.
- **Difficulty:** each opponent is a step harder than the last, as Story already does.
